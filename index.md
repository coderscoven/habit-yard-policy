# Privacy Policy for Habit Yard

**Last updated:** 10th October, 2026

Habit Yard ("the App") is developed and maintained by Rupiny Eric ("I"), an individual developer based in Kampala, Uganda. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, how it is used and stored, and the choices available to you.

## 1. Data stored on your device
Your habits, niches, check-ins, notes, reminders, achievements and settings are stored in a database on your device. We do not receive this data unless you turn on cloud backup (section 2).

## 2. Data we collect
**Account and cloud backup (optional, Premium only).** If you create an account, we collect your email address. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see them. If you tap "Back up now", your habits, niches and check-in dates are copied to Google Firebase Firestore and linked to your account.

**Purchases.** Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your card or payment details. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives your purchase and subscription status and an anonymous app user ID.

**Diagnostics.**
- *Firebase:* We use Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase to collect crash reports, device model, OS version, app version, an app instance ID, and anonymous usage events (such as screens viewed and features used).

**Notifications.** Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not send push notifications from a server. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your system settings.

## 3. How we use data
To run the app and its features, provide backup and restore, process subscriptions, fix bugs, and understand how the app is used so we can improve it.

## 4. Third-party services
- Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
- Firebase / Google: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

These providers process data under their own policies.

## 5. Sharing
We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers above, and when required by law.

## 6. Retention and deletion
- **Local data:** stays on your device until you delete habits or uninstall the app.
- **Cloud data and account:** in the app go to Settings → Account and cloud backup → **Delete account and cloud data**. This removes your account and backup.
- **Request deletion by email:** write to ruperic25@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" from the address linked to your account. We will respond within 1-2 days.
- Diagnostic data is kept according to the retention settings of the providers above.

## 7. Children
The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Security
Cloud data is protected by account authentication and access rules, and is transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of storage is completely secure.

## 9. Your rights
Depending on your location and applicable data protection laws, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data; object to or restrict its processing; withdraw consent where applicable; and request a copy of your data or lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority. Your rights may vary depending on the data protection laws that apply to you. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, email ruperic25@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy Request".
We respect applicable data protection laws, including Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.

## 10. Changes
We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change, and significant changes will be noted in the app or on this page.
